## 🚨 Reporting a Vulnerability

We take security vulnerabilities seriously. If you find a security issue, please report it through the following process:

### 🔍 **How to Report**
1. **📩 Contact Us:**  
   - Submit an issue with **[Security]** in the title (if non-sensitive).  
   - Email us at **abhranildutta1903@gmail.com** for confidential reports.  

2. **⏳ Response Time:**  
   - We aim to acknowledge reports within **48 hours**.  
   - Fixes for critical issues will be prioritized immediately.  

3. **🔒 Responsible Disclosure:**  
   - Please do not disclose vulnerabilities publicly until we release a fix.  
   - Ethical disclosure is highly appreciated!
